# Kill it | RIPPS2
-# RIP PS2, the ELF killer. ELF-sufficient, never ELF-destructive.

**A whole PS2 front end in one 3 MB .elf**, and it just hit **beta**. Your games, discs, memory cards, files, themes and your console's own sounds in one place, with glass towers drawn live by the PS2 behind it all.

### 💿 Launch Disc
PS2, PS1 and DVD discs, named before you boot them. Audio CDs play right on the page while the towers turn into a spectrum analyzer.
### 🎮 Storage
Every drive in one list: USB, HDD, iLink, MX4SIO, MMCE, network. Cover art fetched by the PS2 itself, info pages you tip like a case in your hand, Starred Games, a random pick on L2 + R2, 720p and 1080i.
### 🗂️ Memory Files
A real file manager across cards, USB and HDD: copy, paste, clean replace. VMCs open like folders, PSU in and out, pictures, text, AVI video, ISO and ZSO games straight from the browser, and 3D save icons, lit and animated. Square on a card opens **MCKILLA**, the card manager.
### 🎨 Themes
**RIPGRID** (covers flip over to their backs) and **RIPFLOW** built in, Adapt and Adapt Rx in the pack, six color themes. Hold SELECT to hop themes, or make your own: no rebuild.
### 🔊 Sound from your own console
Your PS2's own browser sounds and its 5:31 menu music, ripped from its BIOS right on the console. No one's sounds ship in the ELF.
### 🧰 All on board
Neutrino, POPSTARTER and Ember ride inside and install themselves. **BIBLE** boots the PS2 straight into RIPPS2. **SpiritofRA** brings RetroAchievements (through xeRAbora on your PC).
