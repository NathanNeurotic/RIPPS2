**Coming next: build 114**
- **All Games at once:** RIPPS2 keeps one list of every game beside your settings, so All Games is whole the moment RIPPS2 starts, before a single drive answers. Drives left on Manual stay instant: pick a game and RIPPS2 starts its drive, then the game.
- **Hide a game:** Triangle > Hide Game. It disappears from every list and lives in Settings > Game Sources > Hidden Games until you show it again. The hidden list stays on the drive, so it follows the drive.
- **Delete a game completely:** the image, its settings, art and cheats, and on an APA drive its whole partition (your other partitions untouched). Saves and RIPSHOT shots stay unless you tick them. Two screens and START, as in the HDD Manager.
