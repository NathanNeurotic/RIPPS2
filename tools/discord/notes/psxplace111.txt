And a short lesson for v1.1.1: RIPPS2 rips your own console's sounds and menu music from its BIOS (Settings > Audio Settings > BIOS Sounds, then BIOS Music). The video starts from a fresh RIPPS2 with no sounds, rips both, and goes through every sound in the order you meet it, so you know what to expect. No music bed, just the console.

[SIZE=4][B]v1.1.1 (build 111)[/B][/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*][B]The ripped menu music plays.[/B] BIOS Music writes a 24 kHz mono WAV, which the PS2's audio driver does not play, so the menu stayed silent. Any 16-bit WAV in your AUDIO folder now plays as 48 kHz stereo.
[*][B]Your music on every theme:[/B] Adapt and Adapt Rx play your pick (or the track called Menu) when the theme has no music of its own.
[*][B]Sounds no longer reload on every theme change[/B] (the SELECT hold included): only a changed file reloads them.
[*][B]Save Settings To keeps a drive with a memory card in[/B], and choosing Memory Card again sticks.
[*]The favourites star on its title's centreline, the Settings highlight centred on its word.
[/LIST]

[LIST]
[*][B]Every drive lists at once:[/B] MMCE, USB, the exFAT HDD, MX4SIO, iLink and SMB keep their last list and show it the moment they answer, then check the drive again while you are idle. The APA HDD's own cache is on.
[*][B]Save Settings To[/B] on a memory card, the internal HDD, a USB drive, MX4SIO, iLink, MMCE or a folder you type.
