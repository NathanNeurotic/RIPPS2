# Build 113 is RIPPS2 v1.1.3 beta: the Adapt sidebar, older OPL themes shown as they were made, a smaller ELF that uses your own POPSTARTER and Ember, and Settings put in order.

## Get it
- [Download the latest RIPPS2.elf](https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2/releases/latest/download/RIPPS2.elf) (this link always gets the newest build), [RIPPS2-languages.zip](https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2/releases/latest/download/RIPPS2-languages.zip) for your language, and [the theme pack](https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2-themes/releases/latest/download/RIPPS2-themes.zip) (edition 113: get it for the sidebar)

## The sidebar
- **Adapt and Adapt Rx put the three categories in their side bar** as glyphs: press Left on the list, Up and Down to open each one's drawer, Cross to go. Storage's drawer lists your sources; Circle opens it straight away.
- **Your choice:** Settings > Interface > Colors and More > Category Layout (Theme, Across the Top, Sidebar), on any theme.

## Older themes
- **A theme made for OPL shows Storage alone**, as it was made to: Launch Disc and Memory Files move to the START menu, and L1 / R1 turn the list's pages. RIPPS2's own themes and the Adapt pack keep all three.

## Smaller ELF
- **POPSTARTER and Ember are no longer inside RIPPS2.elf** (3.2 MB down to 2.7 MB). Your own copies are found where RIPPS2 always looked (POPSTARTER at the Settings path, in POPS, APPS or the top folder, or on a memory card; Ember in the drive's EMBER folder). A missing one tells you the path it checked. Neutrino and BIBLE still ride inside.

## Settings
- **Moved where they belong:** Hints and Glyphs under Interface, the In-Game Menu and the read-ahead caches under Game Launching, and **Game Sources > Cache** with Fast Cache (your saved game lists, on by default), Cache Game List and Automatic Refresh.
- **RIPDISC:** the SELECT hold fills twelve notches now.

## Test list
- **Sidebar on Adapt:** Left, Up, Down, Cross through all three drawers. Smooth, or any stutter when a drawer opens?
- **Storage's drawer:** does it list every source you have (USB, HDD, MMCE, MX4SIO, SMB, Apps), each name whole?
- **Circle on Storage** opens the sources: does Cross on one switch to it?
- **Category Layout = Across the Top** on Adapt: the old bar back as before?
- **An OPL theme** you still have: Storage alone, Launch Disc and Memory Files in START, and back out to Storage?
- **A PS1 game with POPSTARTER** from your own copy: does it start? If not, what path does the message name?
- **A PS1 game with Ember** (BIN and CUE in EMBER/games): does it start with your own ember.elf?
- **Fast Cache off** (Game Sources > Cache): every list read fresh from its drive at boot, and no saved list shown first?
- **Fast Cache on**, power cycle: your lists back as quickly as in v1.1.2?
- **In-Game Menu** from its new place under Game Launching: still opens in game, IGR Path kept?
- **Hints and Glyphs** under Interface: your old choices kept after the update?
- **RIPDISC** with a disc in: the notches fill evenly and HOLD stays clear of them?
- **720p or 1080i** with the sidebar: glyphs and drawers sharp and in place?
